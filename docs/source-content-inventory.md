### Reference and checklist notes

#### 1.1  In-person only meeting or event checklist

- Use for meetings and events where all participants attend in the same physical location.

#### 1.2  Virtual only meeting or event checklist

- Use for meetings and events where all participants join remotely.

#### 1.3  Hybrid meeting or event checklist

- Use for meetings and events with both in-person and remote participants.

#### 2.1  Audience of 1–25 — checklist

- Use for 1–25 participants. Add session-feature and additional-consideration checklists as applicable; small does not mean low risk.

#### 2.2  Training, workshops, and presentations - checklist

- Use for: training sessions, workshops, briefings, demonstrations, and other sessions where content, slides, or participant learning are central.

#### 2.3  Larger meeting (26 to 100 participants) - checklist

- Use for 26–100 participants. Add the high-risk or testing checklist and any selected session features and considerations.

#### 2.4  Town hall, organization-wide session, or audience of 101 or more — checklist

- Use for 101 or more participants, or a town hall or organization-wide session at any size. Add selected session features and considerations.

#### 2.5  Public, formal, or high-risk events checklist

- Use for: public events, high-profile events, formal sessions, technical briefings, legal or policy-heavy events, bilingual events, interpreted events, and recorded events.

#### 3.1  Core accessibility checklist

- Included in every planning path.

#### 3.2  Accessibility contact and follow-up checklist

- Included in every planning path.

#### 3.3  Bilingual and official languages checklist

- Use when the meeting or event is bilingual, interpreted, or involves both official languages.

#### 3.4  Large meetings, public, or high-risk events checklist

- Added for 26 or more participants, town halls or organization-wide sessions, public/formal sessions, explicit high-risk content, or recording the session. Sharing notes alone does not trigger this checklist.

#### 3.5  Visual content checklist

- Added when visual content is selected. Pre-recorded media has its own checklist with instructions for audio-only and video content.
- Note: the delivery format and event type checklists above may also include context-specific reminders to describe visuals (for example, printed materials in a room, or content shared on screen). This item is a general reminder shown whenever visual content applies, and is not meant to replace those more specific items.

#### 3.6  Recording and follow-up materials checklist

- Added for recording or shared follow-up materials. Recording-specific actions apply only when recording is selected; official-record actions also require the Public/formal session feature.

#### 3.7  Indigenous, cultural, and community considerations checklist

- Added automatically when your planning path includes Indigenous, cultural, or community considerations.

#### 3.8  Accessibility supports checklist

- Added when accessibility supports are selected. Apply only the instructions for services you will use; online platform and lobby checks apply to virtual or hybrid delivery.

#### 3.9  Presenter coordination checklist

- Added when Multiple presenters is selected.

#### 3.10  Accessible interaction checklist
